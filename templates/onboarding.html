Welcome to School Day Grid

Build a rotating school-day calendar that stays correct when holidays, snow days, and other closures interrupt the sequence. School Day Grid works by itself; Home Assistant and external calendars are optional.

  • Your schedule lives locally in SQLite.
  • Rotations can be any length: A/B, 5-day, 6-day, or custom.
  • You can import and review “No School” dates from district ICS calendars.
  • You can subscribe to the finished calendar from other apps later.
